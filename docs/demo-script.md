# Demo Script

Hi, I’m Mohaned, and this is Ticket2Fix — an AI Support-to-Code Assistant powered by IBM Bob.

The problem is that support tickets are often vague and incomplete. Developers receive reports like: “After resetting password, users cannot log in. The page reloads but no error is shown.”

Ticket2Fix converts this kind of ticket into a developer-ready task.

The app generates a clean bug summary, reproduction steps, expected and actual behavior, likely affected modules, debugging checklist, acceptance criteria, and suggested tests.

IBM Bob was used as the AI development partner to understand the repository, improve the workflow, generate documentation, suggest test cases, and review the code.

Now I will paste a realistic support ticket into the app.

The app generates a complete developer-ready analysis, including likely affected areas such as authentication service, password reset controller, login form, and token/session logic.

The business value is simple: Ticket2Fix reduces back-and-forth between support and developers, improves ticket quality, speeds up debugging, and helps teams deliver fixes faster.

Ticket2Fix turns messy support tickets into developer-ready action with IBM Bob.
